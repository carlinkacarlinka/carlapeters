Display Neuke

Archives, as repositories of memory, preserve valuable insights into everyday life, cultural traditions, societal identities, work practices, and the evolution of individuals and institutions. The extensive digitisation is transforming these collections, yet the resulting databases are often not user-friendly or widely accessible to diverse audiences.

The website “Display Neuke” makes the previously unpublished estate of German photojournalist Angela Neuke (1943–1997) from the collection of the LVR-LandesMuseum Bonn digitally and interactively accessible.

By contextualizing the material, the analogue archive becomes a living place of knowledge transfer. The website was created as part of a bachelor's thesis.
