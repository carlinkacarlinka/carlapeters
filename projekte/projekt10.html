Pause vom Patriarchat

Illustrations for a seminar series “Pause from Patriarchy” at the queer-feminist educational space lila_bunt in Zülpich. Lila_bunt is a collectively run organisation that primarily offers training, educational leave programs, seminars, networking events, as well as political and cultural events for FLINTA* people.

Lila_bunt aims to be as inclusive as possible, continuously rethinking and practicing the importance of feminist educational spaces through the participation of many people, generations, and perspectives.
