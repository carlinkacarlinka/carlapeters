Info

Carla Peters is shifting between graphic design and social mediation.

She creates her projects using drawing, coding and crafting. She focuses on hybrid publishing and explores ways to create tangible and playful online and offline spaces.

She currently works as part of the queerfeminist organization and collective lila_bunt, where she develops public relations and manages social media. She is also developing the project „Care verbindet“ and lila_raum a space for young women and girls in North-Rhine-Westphalia to connect with others, and actively counter isolation and discrimination faced by queer people in rural areas.

Driven by a strong commitment to politically engaged design, much of her work revolves around feminist history, community care and archives.

This website was developed in HTML/CSS by Carla Peters in 2026. Typefaces used: Amiamie by Byebyebinary <3
